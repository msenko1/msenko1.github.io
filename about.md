I am a 2nd year PhD student in the [Stanford Department of Linguistics](https://linguistics.stanford.edu/ "Stanford Linguistics"), currently advised by [Rob Podesva](https://stanford.edu/~podesva/), [Meghan Sumner](https://profiles.stanford.edu/meghan-sumner), and [Dan Jurafsky](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/). My research is supported by an [EDGE Doctoral Fellowship](https://vpge.stanford.edu/fellowships-funding/EDGE "EDGE Doctoral Fellowship") from the Stanford Office of the Vice Provost for Graduate Education. I am interested in variation in language production, and what within- and across-speaker patterns can tell us about sociolinguistic cognition.

My previous research focused on variation in the uptake of (pro)nominal forms on Twitter and in the stressed vowel of *CHICAGO*. I received a B.S./M.A. in Linguistics & Communication Studies from Northwestern University in 2022, where I worked with [Rob Voigt](https://robvoigt.faculty.ucdavis.edu/ "Rob's Webpage") and [Annette D'Onofrio](https://sites.northwestern.edu/donofrio/ "Annette's Webpage"). I worked as a teacher for three years prior to starting my PhD, first in Taiwan through the [Fulbright](https://www.fulbright.org.tw/ "Fulbright Taiwan") program and later in New York City.
